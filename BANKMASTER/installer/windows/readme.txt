BANKMASTER BY KALOX 2.0.0 - 4-IN-1 MASTERING SUITE
Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit

INPUT > EQ > SATURATION > COMPRESSOR > LIMITER > OUTPUT in a single plug-in.

=== ENGLISH ===
Components (choose on the "Select Components" page):
  VST3 plug-in   -> C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\BANKMASTER.vst3
  Standalone app -> C:\Program Files\Kalox Audio\BANKMASTER\BANKMASTER.exe
No other files are installed and no other plug-in is modified.
After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Ableton Live: Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan).
Uninstall: Settings > Apps > "BANKMASTER BY KALOX" > Uninstall (or Start menu > Kalox Audio > Uninstall BANKMASTER).
Your presets in %APPDATA%\KALOX\BANKMASTER are kept.
Upgrading from 1.0: run this installer; it replaces 1.0. Projects that use BANKMASTER keep loading it
(same plug-in IDs). In Ableton Live the plug-in is now listed under "Kalox Audio" after a rescan.
This installer is not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC".
Click "More info" > "Run anyway" only if you obtained the file from the official Kalox Audio source.
BANKMASTER does not need any third-party plug-in to run.
