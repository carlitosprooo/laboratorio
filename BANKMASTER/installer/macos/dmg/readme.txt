BANKMASTER BY KALOX 2.0.0 - 4-IN-1 MASTERING SUITE
macOS Universal (Apple Silicon arm64 + Intel x86_64) - macOS 10.15 or later

=== ENGLISH ===

INSTALL
1. Double-click "Install BANKMASTER.pkg".
2. Click "Customize" to choose the formats:
     VST3        -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/BANKMASTER.vst3
     Audio Unit  -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/BANKMASTER.component
     Standalone  -> /Applications/BANKMASTER.app
   Nothing else is written. Other plug-ins are not modified.
3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan).

UPGRADING FROM 1.0
Install over 1.0: the same three items are replaced. Projects that use BANKMASTER keep
loading it with their settings (same plug-in IDs). The maker is now shown as "Kalox Audio":
in Ableton Live it appears under "Kalox Audio" after a rescan (hold Option while clicking
Rescan for a full rescan). If an Audio Unit host still shows the old name, log out and in.

SECURITY NOTICE
This version is NOT signed with an Apple Developer ID and NOT notarized.
macOS will refuse to open the installer with a double-click. To proceed:
right-click (Control-click) "Install BANKMASTER.pkg" > Open > Open,
or System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".
Only do this if you obtained the file from the official Kalox Audio source.

UNINSTALL
Double-click "Uninstall BANKMASTER.command" (asks for confirmation and your
administrator password). It removes only the three items listed above and the
installer receipts. Your presets in ~/Library/Application Support/KALOX/BANKMASTER
are kept.

FIRST USE
Insert ONE instance of BANKMASTER on the master track. It opens with the preset
"KALOX MASTER - ORIGINAL". Play the loudest section and press LEARN (bottom bar)
twice to set the input level to the reference (-20 LUFS).
BANKMASTER does not need any third-party plug-in to run.

=== PORTUGUES ===

INSTALAR
1. Clique duas vezes em "Install BANKMASTER.pkg".
2. Clique em "Personalizar" para escolher os formatos:
     VST3        -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/BANKMASTER.vst3
     Audio Unit  -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/BANKMASTER.component
     Standalone  -> /Applications/BANKMASTER.app
   Nenhum outro arquivo e gravado. Outros plugins nao sao modificados.
3. Faca o Rescan dos plugins na DAW (Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan).

ATUALIZANDO DA 1.0
Instale por cima da 1.0: os mesmos tres itens sao substituidos. Projetos que usam o BANKMASTER
continuam abrindo com seus ajustes (mesmos IDs do plugin). O fabricante agora aparece como
"Kalox Audio": no Ableton Live o plugin fica em "Kalox Audio" depois do Rescan (segure Option ao
clicar em Rescan para uma varredura completa). Se um host AU ainda mostrar o nome antigo, faca
logout e login.

AVISO DE SEGURANCA
Esta versao NAO e assinada com Apple Developer ID e NAO e notarizada.
O macOS vai recusar abrir o instalador com duplo clique. Para prosseguir:
botao direito (Control-clique) em "Install BANKMASTER.pkg" > Abrir > Abrir,
ou Ajustes do Sistema > Privacidade e Seguranca > "Abrir Mesmo Assim".
Faca isso somente se obteve o arquivo da fonte oficial Kalox Audio.

DESINSTALAR
Clique duas vezes em "Uninstall BANKMASTER.command" (pede confirmacao e a senha de
administrador). Remove somente os tres itens acima e os recibos do instalador.
Seus presets em ~/Library/Application Support/KALOX/BANKMASTER sao mantidos.

PRIMEIRO USO
Coloque UMA instancia do BANKMASTER na master. Ele abre no preset
"KALOX MASTER - ORIGINAL". Toque o trecho mais alto e pressione LEARN (barra inferior)
duas vezes para ajustar o nivel de entrada a referencia (-20 LUFS).
O BANKMASTER nao precisa de nenhum plugin de terceiros para funcionar.
